## Conversion tracking (already on the site)

The site fires GA4 events: `book_click`, `chat_booking_confirm`, `contact_form_submit`.
In Google Ads: Tools → Conversions → Import from Google Analytics (link GA4 property
`G-T13Y9WJ53P`) → pick those events. Then switch bidding to *Maximise Conversions*
once ≥30 conversions/month.

## Campaign settings (starting point)

- **Geo:** Singapore first (+ your priority markets as separate campaigns — different
  budgets let you learn where demand actually is)
- **Language:** English
- **Bidding:** start Maximise Clicks with a modest cap → switch to Maximise Conversions
  after the GA4 import above has data
- **Ad rotation:** let Google optimise after ~2 weeks
- **Landing page:** the homepage — it routes to Calendly, the HubSpot form and Orbit chat
